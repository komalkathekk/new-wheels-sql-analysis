# New Wheels - SQL Business Analytics

## Project Overview
Analyzed vehicle sales and customer feedback data for New Wheels, a vehicle resale company, to generate quarterly business reports and identify key business trends using SQL.

## Business Objective
Develop SQL-based reports to help leadership monitor sales performance, customer satisfaction, revenue, and shipping efficiency.

## Tools & Technologies
- SQL
- MySQL
- Microsoft Excel

## SQL Concepts Used
- Joins
- Aggregate Functions
- GROUP BY
- CASE Statements
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Window Functions (RANK(), LAG())
- Date Functions

## Key Business Insights
- Customer ratings declined steadily across all four quarters.
- Net revenue and total orders decreased consistently throughout the year.
- Shipping time increased significantly, impacting customer satisfaction.
- Generated quarterly KPIs and business recommendations to support executive decision-making.

## Business Recommendations
- Improve shipping efficiency.
- Strengthen after-sales support.
- Introduce customer retention programs.
- Optimize discount strategies.
- Monitor KPIs through executive dashboards.
